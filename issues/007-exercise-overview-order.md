# 007 - Bench press variants appear near the top of Exercises

## What happens

Paused Bench Press, Spoto Bench Press, and Larsen Bench Press appear near the start of the
Chest group when opening the Exercises overview (NAV-04).

## What should happen

Put these three entries after every other match in the Exercises overview. The unfiltered
view shows them in a final Bench press variants section. Search and filters still decide
which exercises match, and the other entries keep their existing order. This applies to
List, Cards, and Grid.

## Reproduce

Open Exercises and check the first Chest rows. At 375 x 667, scroll to the bottom and check
that all three variants appear there. Tap Chest and check that they are the last matches.
Search for Spoto and tap the result to open its details.
